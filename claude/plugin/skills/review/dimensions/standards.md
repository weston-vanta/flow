# Standards

**Asks:** does the change follow the repo's own guidance: its `CLAUDE.md` or `AGENTS.md` files, its glossary, its ADRs and its templates?

**A finding must show:** the guidance it breaks, by location (file and heading or line), quoted.

Your taste is not a standard. Where the repo is silent there is no finding; where two pieces of guidance conflict, say so as a non-blocking finding naming both.
